Join Us

We welcome climbers of all abilities, whether you are just starting out or have been on the rock for years.

Membership Benefits

Through club membership you will get the following benefits:
  • BMC Membership through club affiliation
  • Regular outdoor meets on Wednesday evenings across the county
  • Regular indoor meets during the winter months
  • Access to our club Whatsapp Chat, for organising club and adhoc meets
Through BMC club membership you will get the following benifits:
  • Access to BMC events and festivals
  • Personal accident insurance
  • Combined liability insurance
  • Discounts at outdoors retailers and discounted entry at some climbing walls
More details of the benifits BMC membership brings can be found on the BMC website.
